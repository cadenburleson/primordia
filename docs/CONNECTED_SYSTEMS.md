# Connected cellular systems — v0.12

This is the active application in `primordia/`, at http://127.0.0.1:5194/. It extends the common physical habitat used by the main run and its passive and scarcity assays. The old supplied-body comparison labs remain separate. This is an artificial-life model with physical mechanisms; it is not a full simulation of terrestrial biology.

## Implemented together

Founders still receive four genes and finite inventories. Mutations can add, remove and regulate 32 material-production reactions. There are 28 local regulatory inputs, up to 48 genes per chromosome, and up to 64,000 living cells. No brain, skeletal layout, gait, eye, organ, sex or complexity reward is assigned to a founder.

- **Development and heredity:** material-splitting division, diploid expression, chromosome copying and mutation, polarity-dependent division geometry, a reproductive origin and developmental depth, recombinant gametes, physical-contact fertilization, and two-parent ancestry. Produced replication-repair material can reduce copying errors at an ATP cost. Its local expression can differ among cells; it does not establish a guaranteed protected germline. Membrane-recognition molecules modulate contact binding and fusion through inherited regulatory coefficients. Gametes, embryos and daughter cells inherit actual existing inventories.
- **Fluid compartments:** produced barriers and matrix retain water taken from the planet's finite water pool. Internal compartments hold the same finite fuel, oxidized waste, minerals, signals and detritus as the environment. Permeability, concentration gradients and produced transporters move material across membranes. Attached compartments exchange fluid through pressure-driven reduced Poiseuille conductance and donor-bounded advection/diffusion. Polarity and barriers can restrict reverse flow. Contraction and hydraulic pumping consume ATP. This is a graph of resolved compartment volumes, not a meshed anatomical lumen or a full volumetric Navier–Stokes solver.
- **Feeding, respiration and waste:** cells can absorb internal fuel, digest internal detritus, filter oxidized waste, and exchange a finite available/spent redox carrier with their local environment. Gas-exchange material supports additional carrier-limited respiration. Light-powered regeneration spends tracked photon energy. The redox pair is a reduced chemical proxy, not molecular oxygen/carbon-dioxide stoichiometry. The original reduced respiration pathway remains available. Excretion, decomposition and death redistribute conserved material.
- **Support, fat and advanced movement:** stored lipids, precipitated minerals, secreted matrix and crosslinked fibers affect inertia, volume, stiffness and material transport. Fiber orientation changes directional contact stiffness. Mineral remodeling and contact-rest remodeling depend on local load/damage and produced enzymes; remodeling spends ATP. Material toughness and stiffness affect fracture/damage. Signal-driven contraction generates internal forces and angular actuation; water and terrain supply physical contacts. The implementation contains no prescribed target velocity, walking animation, locomotion reward or adult skeleton. These are reduced material mechanics rather than real bone/muscle histology or calibrated biomechanics.
- **Sensing and adaptive signaling:** local chemical receptors, membrane light orientation/contrast, mechanical channels, local injury and internal pressure feed regulatory and excitable dynamics. Directed excitatory/inhibitory contacts require produced signaling material and intact physical bonds. Transmission and local covariance-based plasticity cost ATP. Contact degree normalizes incoming drive. Weights survive saving the current body; newly formed contacts begin from genetic coefficients. Fertilization resets learned state. This is a reduced adaptive network; a brain or intelligent behavior has not been established.
- **Compositional material chemistry:** a produced assembler can create variable sequences of four abstract, equal-mass monomer classes. Gene coefficients provide a template; existing polymers can template new assembly with copying changes. Composition and adjacent motifs determine sticky, charged and rigid material properties used in adhesion, membrane permeability and stiffness. Synthesis uses fuel and ATP; turnover returns fuel. Each cell keeps at most 12 species, with excess variants hydrolyzed back into fuel. This expands the combinations of material identities; it does not discover arbitrary valid real chemistry, reaction pathways, atomistic bonds or quantum energetics.
- **Material recycling and particles:** some dead-body detritus and minerals become visible 3D particles with finite inventories. Flow, settling and terrain move them. Produced digestive and mineral-binding enzymes can consume them on physical contact; gradual dissolution returns their material to the environmental pools. Up to 4,096 particles are represented individually; overflow remains in the existing solute pools. Particle transport is a reduced settling/drag model, not a resolved solid/fluid momentum solver. Matrix-digesting enzymes can consume another cell's supporting material. Membrane-lysis catalysts can recycle a cell's own biomass, allowing local removal during development without an anatomical template.
- **Planetary setting:** existing day/night, seasons, rain, snow, melting, evaporation, volcanism, altered terrain, finite water, mineral inputs, radial gravity and curved surface flow remain integrated. Globe circumferences of 1,024, 8,192 and 64,000 model units are added. The last is 1,000 times the original 64-unit habitat span. Cell dimensions remain approximately 0.44 units initially. Fields above 256 units use a capped 160-by-80 planetary grid: these are coarser environment fields, not a fully resolved microscopic planetary chemistry model. Chemical gradients within a coarse site remain unresolved; direct cell contacts and internal body processes retain individual 3D positions. Large spans currently require spherical geometry. A local starting origin keeps founders near one another in a huge world.

## How to explore the build

New habitat presets now start on a 1,024-unit spherical circumference with 256 founders in one local origin and an 8,192-cell ceiling. Compared with the screenshot's 256-unit circumference, this gives four times the circumference and sixteen times the spherical surface area while retaining initial cell diameter. The setup also offers 8,192 and 64,000 circumferences: respectively 1,024 and 62,500 times the surface area of the 256-unit globe. These are habitat size ratios, not measured biodiversity or carrying-capacity improvements. The cell ceiling is not raised automatically.

Choose a larger **World size**, keep **One local origin** if you want nearby founders, and use **Create world**. Save an existing run first; changing setup does not expand a live world's geometry. **Restore previous world** retains the existing held-world behavior. Large worlds now open near the first living population at actual biological scale. The microscope toolbar button returns to a local population view; the expand button opens a world overview. In a large-world overview, fixed-screen-size dots mark actual cell locations and can be clicked to inspect that region. These dots do not represent cell dimensions. **Follow this lifeform** fits and follows one selected body. The old fixed-density spherical fog could obscure large worlds and has been removed.

World creation is one sidebar flow: choose a habitat, world size, starting cells and a living-cell limit, then **Create world**. Seed, geometry and placement are under **Seed & placement**. Choosing a habitat edits the pending setup and does not replace the live world. **View & run settings** separately controls the current cell limit and resource overlay.

Experiments are under the header's **Labs** button. Current-engine passive mechanics and scarce-resource experiments start fresh founders using the live world's settings and hold the existing run. They do not unlock mechanisms. Earlier supplied-body and trait experiments are grouped under **Legacy comparisons**. An active experiment shows its model and a **Return to main world** button above the display; world creation is hidden during the experiment to keep its held world clear. These interface changes leave the main physical tick and inherited mechanisms intact.

Graphics now use simple materials without postprocessing, bloom, fog, shadows, decorative stars/rain, synthetic cell nuclei or automatic rotation. The renderer draws at most 30 FPS, or 15 FPS at Max; the specimen view is capped at 15 FPS. Unchanged paused views draw only when interaction or new data changes the scene, and hidden views skip drawing. Physical body matrices update from snapshots, not per-frame interpolation. Planet-surface mesh updates are limited to once per second. The graphics resolution button adjusts pixel density only. These are rendering changes: the physical weather, materials, chemistry and biological mechanisms remain in the simulation. No runtime performance claim is made without user testing.

The CPU scheduling changes have also been carried into this project: a six-millisecond batch target checked between complete fixed ticks, at least 35 milliseconds idle after work, no periodic stepping/snapshots while hidden, and no repeated unchanged paused snapshots. **Max** still uses those limits; achieved speed reports completed ticks. A single slow Rust tick can exceed the deadline, so this is not a hard CPU-percentage cap. Hidden time is not simulated when the tab returns. No simulation tests, browser runs or performance benchmarks were performed for this packaging revision; only builds were requested. Runtime testing belongs to the user.

Run `npm run dev`, or open `Primordia.command`, then use port 5194. Create a physical world with a chosen starting count, seed, ceiling, span and distribution. Existing speed choices include 1,000×, 10,000× and Max; the actual rate depends on the machine and simulated population. Save before long runs or changing experiments. The main habitat, passive mechanics assay and scarce-resource assay use the same connected engine.

The inspector shows inherited chromosomes and actual material stores, including internal water/solutes/gases, matrix/fibers and assembled sequences. Developing-body statistics report measured flow, absorption, filtration, respiration, remodeling, signaling and reproduction. These are observations, not organ unlocks. A connected material region, compartment, or adaptive network does not by itself establish an anatomical organ or useful behavior.

## Build versus evolutionary evidence

The connected implementation is being packaged before another broad simulation-test checkpoint, as requested. The earlier v0.11 checkpoint passed 90 native tests and its initial browser/WASM checks; those results do not validate these v0.12 additions. Long-run behavior, novel evolved organs, advanced movement, intelligence and anatomical sexual reproduction remain unproven until this version is explored and tested. No timetable guarantees them. The remaining research work in [LIFE_ENGINE_ROADMAP.md](LIFE_ENGINE_ROADMAP.md) includes volumetric anatomy, stronger developmental stability, realistic chemical feasibility, refined mechanics, resolved optical transport, and true adaptive planet-to-cell resolution.

Previous vocabulary arrays are padded with zeros. Old molecules and genes are preserved. New compartment, gas-budget, particle and material state defaults inactive. Save validation includes modeled organic/mineral/chemical-energy, biological water and redox-carrier balances. These checks are implemented guards, not a claim that the new dynamics have already passed a validation suite. Save continuation is version-specific; changing mechanisms changes future trajectories.

## Ecology and evidence tools

[Observation and matched-checkpoint assays](OBSERVATION_AND_ASSAYS.md) describes the new Ecology/Evidence tabs, real per-cell material fluxes and feeding records, area-normalized resource inspection, optional patchy food/shade, and user-started mechanism ablations from the same saved state. These measurements do not award traits or certify organs, walking or intelligence. Resource fields remain coarse on large planets. Existing worlds retain diffuse settings when the new configuration is absent. Current new-world presets use patchy food and shade. Static/build checks are not runtime validation.
