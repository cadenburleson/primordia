# Inherited development and reproduction — v0.11

The current app runs from `primordia/` on port 5194. Earlier prototypes are preserved in `archive/`. It extends the same physical engine used by the main habitat, passive assay and scarcity assay. Supplied adult-body labs remain legacy comparisons. This is a bounded artificial-life research model, not a simulation of everything in biology or a prediction of the origin of life.

## Integrated mechanisms

Ordinary founders still have four genes and no anatomy. Twenty-one material-production reactions and twenty local promoter inputs are now available to mutation. The five additions produce photoreceptors, adaptive contact polymers, gametic division catalysts, membrane fusogens and polar scaffolds. Each protein inventory is made from existing fuel and maintained through the chemical and ATP budget. Adding a reaction to the vocabulary does not insert its gene into a founder or guarantee its evolution. Engineering bounds remain 48 genes per chromosome, six contacts per cell and at most 64,000 living cells; this is a bounded search space.

Photoreceptors sense effective light at the actual cell location, modulated by membrane orientation and attached-neighbor shading. Sensing spends ATP. This is a scalar local detector, not an image-forming eye or optical ray tracer. A polar scaffold biases a division plane along the cell's inherited orientation; it does not prescribe limbs, positions or adult size. Promoters may now respond to light, signed contact-network input, division depth since a reproductive origin, and the local difference between chemical signals. Spatial chemistry, differing neighbors and developmental history can therefore give related cells different expression patterns.

Cells with synthesized adaptive contact material and excitable channels can establish directed signaling edges over intact adhesive contacts. Genomic coefficients seed excitatory or inhibitory weights. Transmission and bounded activity-dependent plasticity spend ATP. Excitation responds to local receptors, light and network input; the existing contraction mechanisms read excitation and recovery. Motion still comes from paid internal forces interacting with water and terrain. There is no assigned target velocity, reward for locomotion, supplied neural topology, brain region or gait. Adaptive weights are transient state: they are saved for the same body, but new cells start new contacts from genomic coefficients. A fertilized cell resets excitation, sensory memory and learned contacts.

Material-paid division can produce a detached gamete if gametic catalyst and fusogen material exist. Gametes cannot perform ordinary fission, establish adhesive tissues or copy contact genes. Their inventories and ATP come from the producing cell. Haploid founders can bud haploid gametes. Diploid producers make a haploid recombinant chromosome from their two chromosomes. Compatible gametes from different reproductive origins may fuse only on physical contact, with fusogen material and sufficient ATP. Compatibility is a designed overlap measure over reaction genes, and same-origin fusion is excluded; this is a model rule, not a universal biological mating rule.

Fusion combines existing stores, spends ATP and creates a single diploid zygote with both parents' chromosomes. Subsequent divisions copy both chromosomes with mutations and average their regulatory expression. The zygote establishes a new reproductive origin; division depth begins again. Both parents and fertilization events are shown separately from horizontal gene transfer. This is a reproductive bottleneck and an abstract ploidy/recombination cycle, not molecular meiosis, germline specification, chromosome homology, fertilization anatomy or a supplied sexual organ.

Mineral support, contractile polymers, lipid reserves, local receptors, excitation, intercellular transport, contact digestion, defenses, repair, excretion, decomposition, photon-powered recycling, weather, volcanism, radial gravity and curved surface transport remain integrated. Material-region counts are observations. Neither a region count nor neural plasticity demonstrates an anatomical organ or intelligence.

## Scientific basis

- [OpenStax: sexual reproduction](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/11-2-sexual-reproduction) and [meiosis](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/11-1-the-process-of-meiosis) describe the biological distinction between haploid gametes, fertilization, diploid growth and chromosome reduction. The model keeps that distinction but substitutes an abstract variable gene list and one crossover.
- [Sims, Evolving Virtual Creatures](https://www.karlsims.com/papers/siggraph94.pdf) shows evolution of coupled morphology and controllers in a designed physical substrate. This ecosystem uses ecological survival and reproduction rather than that paper's locomotion fitness optimizer.
- [Open-Ended Evolution: Perspectives from the OEE Workshop in York](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_A_00210) discusses why population size, duration and a richer search space do not by themselves establish sustained open-ended evolution.

## Remaining work, in dependency order

1. **Reliable inherited morphogenesis.** Demonstrate repeated differentiated body growth through the new reproductive cycle, measure within-body mutation/cooperation, and investigate heritable developmental stability. Current cells have no protected germline or guaranteed body-wide genotype. Contact-connected groups may combine unrelated lineages.
2. **Functional organ architecture.** Add cell-produced extracellular matrix with resolved volume, lumens and membranes; explicitly transported internal fluids, gases and nutrients; pressure/valves and diffusion-distance limits. Demonstrate feeding, circulation, respiration and waste handling by intervention and repeatable development. Current contact regions are not hearts, lungs, guts or kidneys.
3. **Advanced musculoskeletal movement.** Refine physical calibration, soft-tissue volume constraints, anisotropic mineral/fiber mechanics, tendon attachments, fracture and remodeling. Evolve effective sensor-to-motor responses through ecological advantage. Existing mineralized particles and polymer motors are support/actuation affordances, not vertebrate bone and muscle histology.
4. **Sensory organization and memory.** Add local occluded light transport, membrane sensitivity distributions, inhibitory/homeostatic dynamics and longer-lived learning. Measure improved behavior and inherited development; neuron count alone is not intelligence. The current signed contact network is a reduced mechanism.
5. **Reproductive tissues and life histories.** Add heritable germline allocation, gamete maturation/size tradeoffs, barriers and transport, embryonic nutrition, mate-recognition signals and reproductive failure. Anatomical sexual organs must develop from those mechanisms and prove useful, rather than being unlocked at a generation threshold.
6. **Generative chemistry.** Replace enumerated catalyst identities with compositional molecules and local reaction rules tied to energetic/structural constraints. Validate mass, charge and reaction feasibility. An arbitrary new name or random reaction is not novel valid chemistry; realistic chemical discovery is a major separate research problem.
7. **Planet-to-cell scales.** Couple coarse climate and geology to regional ecosystems and microscopic active habitats. Transfer conserved inventories and population/genetic summaries between resolutions. Earth's 12,756 km equatorial diameter is about 1.28 trillion 10-micrometer cell widths ([NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/facts/), [OpenStax cell sizes](https://openstax.org/books/biology/pages/4-2-prokaryotic-cells)). Increasing one global grid is not that architecture. Current world units and field resolution are explicitly model units; cells are not Earth-scale representations.
8. **Evolutionary evidence.** Run multiple ordinary-founder seeds across bottleneck generations, with matched mechanism-disabled controls and quantitative reports of persistence, reproduction, function and novel niches. No automatic rescue or complexity score. Distinguish invented mechanisms, controlled fixtures and observed evolved outcomes.

These are research milestones, not timed anatomical unlocks. Evolution can remain simple, become simpler or go extinct. More iterations cannot produce a structure excluded by the model's state space, and possible structures are not guaranteed to be selected.

## Compatibility and validation

Previous 8/16-input and 10/16-material vocabularies pad to the new sizes with zeros. Existing genes and molecules remain unchanged on migration. New lifecycle, sensing and adaptive-network state defaults to inactive/haploid. Save validation checks chromosomes, lifecycle phases, ancestry, network endpoints/weights, finite state and modeled chemical residuals. Continuation is exact within this engine version; changed vocabulary and dynamics change futures across versions.

Controlled native fixtures test sensing, excitation, plasticity, paid movement coupling, absence of free signaling, chromosome reduction/recombination, gamete budding, contact/compatibility gates, fertilization, inheritance, conservation and state validation. These fixtures deliberately supply mechanisms and are not evolved organisms. WASM integration checks begin with ordinary four-gene founders, report what appears, and test exact continuation and migration. A single integration checkpoint follows the connected implementation batch.
